About

Coaching my boys

I have two sons, 7 and 11, and I've spent years coaching their youth sports teams. Every game meant juggling a clipboard to make sure each kid got fair playing time. The tools out there were paywalled or built for adult leagues, so I built Bench Boss.

Golf

I love the game, and golf trips with friends are one of my favorite traditions. We always wanted a fantasy layer on top of our own rounds, with stakes, trash talk and a live leaderboard. Nothing like that existed, so Your Tour does it now.
